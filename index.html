About Me

I am a Materials Science & Engineering student at Cornell University pursuing combined B.S. and M.S. degrees. My interests lie at the intersection of polymer science, advanced manufacturing, and materials characterization.

Through research in the Laboratory of Soft and Living Materials, I investigate polymer systems, structure-property relationships, and characterization techniques including DMA, DSC, NMR, GPC, and synchrotron analysis.

Beyond research, I enjoy engineering design projects involving composites, CAD, 3D printing, fabrication, and sustainable materials development.
